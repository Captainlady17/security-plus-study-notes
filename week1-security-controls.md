# Week 1 - Security Controls (1.1)

## What I learned:
Security controls are the safety measures used to protect a company from cyberattacks and threats.

### Types of controls (by category):
- Technical - done using technology (firewall, antivirus, passwords)
- Managerial - rules and policies set by management
- Operational - daily actions carried out by people (staff training, ID checks)
- Physical - real-world protection (locks, cameras, fences)

### Types of controls (by purpose):
- Preventive - stops an attack before it happens (example: a locked door)
- Detective - notices an attack while it's happening (example: a security camera)
- Corrective - fixes things after an attack occurs (example: restoring a backup)
- Deterrent - discourages someone from attempting an attack (example: a warning sign)
- Compensating - a backup plan used when the main control isn't possible
- Directive - tells people what they must do (example: "Wear ID badge at all times")

## Simple example I used to understand it:
Protecting a house = lock (preventive) + camera (detective) + fixing a broken window after a break-in (corrective)
